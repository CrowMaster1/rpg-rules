Skill ranks

No starting pool of skill ranks. Free ranks come from the Basis skills and your race (kap. 9); every other rank is bought with coins (Profiency increase). Age can change your start coins (kap. 9).

Stiger man i stats gives retroaktive HP.

Detect Hidden

Dette er en aktiv skill hvor spiller beskriver hvad der kigges efter. Erstatter ofte Spot.

Only rogues have Detect Hidden – it is what a rogue can do with Spot that other classes cannot. Used actively, it adds the rogue's level bonus (Rogue Skills, +½ per rogue level). Passive Spot does not.

Bruges til at finde hemmelige ting, ie. gemte skatte under gulvet. Folk der har brugt move/hide osv. Kan også bruges til at forsøge at overhører andres samtaler. Kan også bruges til forhører folk mhp. at få informationer ud af dem(finde ud af om de lyver).

Backstab

Rogue kan backstabbe personer, der ikke er klar over hvor rogue er.

Hvis man backstabber et monster slås monster Lore DC 12 for common, 15 rare osv. Man kan max får modifier (#DC above) på hvad man slår over slaget.

Der slås et stealth check med modifikationer.

  • Der slås et stealth vs. DC13+SPOT

    • Lykkes slag gives Backstab skade + effekt + melee slag(se nedenfor)

    • Misser slaget men med mindre end 5 dårlig, opdager defender først rogue lige før stikket – der slås initiativ med AoO osv.

    • Misser slaget >5<15 misses. Opdages Rogue 1 secondary move action fra defender og der slås initiativ

    • Misser slag ≥15 eller slås 1. Opdager defender rogue på sikker afstand og kan enten alarmere eller forsøge at suprise rogue(acting check sikkert).
      Dvs cirka 20 m.

  • Hvis man rammer slår rogue normal melee våben to hit + skade

    • Target er suprised(til dette angreb) – der kan laves specials

    • Misser slaget gives forsat backstab dam/effects

  • Backstab med missiles

    • Man kan backstabbe med x-bow eller buer – der skal sigtes i rimelig åben sight (derfor normal stealth slag).

      • #above divideres med 2 på normal range (rundes ned)

      • #above divideres med 4 på long range (rundes ned)

      • Man kan forsøge et point-blank backstab med en (enkelt) hand crossbow. Hvis man prøver med en større x-bow gives -5 på stealth check.

Man kan backstabbe med blackjack.

  • Skade er så ikke permanent.

  • Rogue kan vælge at target istedet for skade slås Health save for at blive bevidstløs.

    • Save er base DC 15 + #above/2

    • Man er out cold i 5-10 minutter. (DM slår 20% chance for at vågne per minut over 5 (kumuleres))

    • Hvis man angribes vågner man.

    • Angrebet giver i øvrigt ikke skade.

Paladin

En paladin er en hellig kriger af en religion. De skal være Lawful jvf at de følger en meget streng individuel kodeks. De har dog meget sjældent behov for at prædike via andet end eksempel. Den primære dominas er good/neutral/evil der dominerer deres handlinger.

De kan dog ved deres gerninger have en meget ensrettende indflydelse på verdenen – det er sjældent pragmatisme der dominerer deres liv.

De er ofte tilhængere af Tyr/Loki, Torm, Bane & Helm eller andre Lawful entities.

Der findes dog andre paladins af de mere Lawful retninger af Lathander og Murkyl.

Ranger

En Ranger er en blanding mellem kriger og druid. Rangers har en kraftig relation til naturen.

De tilbeder ofte Malar, Milikki eller Sylvanus. De behøves ikke kende navnet på den gud de tilbeder.

Ranger kampevner

Kampevner: starts with Leather, Studded and Shield, and weapon groups as the fighter: 3 of choice at level 1, then 1 more per level until all 8 at level 6. Picks from the common list at level 1, 3, 5, 7 … (se Kampevner).

The ranger does not get Favorite Weapon or Fighter styles. Its Gifts of the Wild are its path.

From level 5 a ranger has Trick Shooting II free: it shoots in melee without disadvantage. It does not need Trick Shooting I for it.

Gifts of the Wild

A ranger's bond with the wild gives small, quiet gifts. They are not spells: no cast roll, no slots, no domain, and they cannot be pushed.

Level 1

Read the Trail*

Touch a track or a trail sign. You know how old it is, how many made it and what kind of creature – no roll. For the rest of the day you have +5 Tracking on that trail.

Level 2

Fade into the Green (BA)*

In natural terrain (forest, scrub, rocks, reeds, snow) you have +5 Sneak for 10 minutes, as long as you move at most your combat move per round. Armour DC mod applies as normal.

Level 3

Entangle (primary)*

Roots and grass seize one target you can see within 20 m, on natural ground. Str or Bal save DC 15 (the target's choice), or it cannot take move actions for 2 rounds. It can still attack and parry.

−5 to the DC per size above normal (Large DC 10, Huge DC 5). On stone floors, planks or paving: no effect.

Stinging Swarm (secondary)

Biting insects swarm one creature you can see within 20 m. It makes a Hea save DC 15 or has disadvantage on its attacks until the end of its next turn. Each further swarm on the same creature in one fight: +5 to its save, as with Throw Sand.

Level 4

Eagle Eye Shot (primary)*

One shot with a bow or crossbow: no long-range penalty, range doubled, and the crit range is one wider (16–19). The moving-target penalty still applies.

Level 5

Mark the Quarry (BA)*

Mark one creature you can see. For a day you know its direction within 1 km. For this fight you have +1 to hit and +1 dam with missile weapons against it.

Level 6

Pass Without Trace*

For one day the tracks of the ranger and up to 6 companions are hidden. Tracking to follow them is DC +6.

Level 7

Beast Speech*

For 10 minutes you can calm one natural animal (not a monster) and ask it simple questions about what it has seen nearby. It answers as an animal: "many two-legs, smell of smoke, that way."

Level 8

Barkskin (BA)*

Your skin hardens like bark: +2 abs for 3 rounds. It is natural abs, so with armour it layers as in kap. 4 (Armor & natural abs): +1 on top of armour, +2 without.

Level 9

Wild Stride*

For one day you and up to 6 companions travel through forest, scrub, marsh or snow as fast as on a road (kap. 7 terrain).

Level 10

Call of the Wild (primary)*

One natural animal that lives there – a wolf, a bear, an eagle, not a monster – comes within a few rounds and fights at your side for 10 minutes. Whatever kind it is, it fights with these numbers: 25 HP, AC 12, abs 1, one attack +4 for 1d8+3, move 30 m. It acts right after you and does what you ask.

Level 11

Mark the Quarry II (BA)*

As Mark the Quarry, but +2 to hit and +2 dam with missile weapons against the mark.

Level 12

Call of the Pack (primary)*

Call of the Wild brings two animals, each with the numbers of Call of the Wild.

*Powers marked * restore when the ranger spends at least 8 hours in quiet communion at a place of the wild – an old grove, a spring, a standing stone, a hilltop under open sky – and leaves a fitting offering for what the gift achieved. The ranger has 1 FP the day after.

If there is no such place, any wild spot will do for the 8 hours, but then only one * power restores per day. The ranger has 1 FP the day after. Not in a town or an inn.

Wizards Elemental Bolt (primary+secondary)

Wizards Elemental Throw. Alle 3 bolts skal være same element.

Bolt er under normale missile regler. Dvs der er to-hit hvor man skal ramme personens AC.

Der er abs.

Man kan parry som missile – der kan dog være spalsh damage.

De følger også shooting into melee og alle de andre ting. En troldmand skal min have en hånd fri for at bruge dem – dog med -3 to hit. Hvis man bruger en staff (som fokus, ikke som våben) gives ikke minus.

Man kan også øge skade (per missil) med samme regler (-2 hit for +1 dam).

En troldmand kan sigte efter flere targets. Her deles to-hit op i lige dele (dvs 2 targets, og +8 to hit = +4 to hit på hver. Man kan ikke gå under 0 – så kan man bare ikke ramme. An uneven to-hit is split as the caster chooses: +5 on two targets is +2 and +3; on three targets +1, +2 and +2.

To hit er lig kaster level/2 (rundet op) + int mod.

Bruges spell til at øge antal targets deles denne i grupper sådan at troldmanden ikke skal opdele sin to hit i flere grupper end "basis"

Der er -2 to hit på long range. Som trækkes fra initielt.

Der kan crittes på 17-20, dog er crits tabel med -10. regler for minor crits osv gælder også her. crits er x2 dam. Fumbles slås på magi fumbles.

Man kan ”pushe” sine bolts med spells – der slås for spell per normal rules. Hvis den lykkes gives ekstra effekt (se række). Misser spell har man forsat sine normal bolts (på nær ved fumbles)

Der er ikke uendelige bolts – men i praktiske termer er der nok til at der ikke skal holdes styr på antal. Der er nok omkring 30 om dagen.

Ward (Force)

A wizard or sorcerer with Force at domain level 2 (Novice) or more can wrap themself in a field of magical force. Like the bolt it is not a spell: no cast roll, no slot.

  • Raising it: primary action, one hand free. A staff held as a focus is fine, as for the bolt.

  • Keeping it: a secondary action every round, at most 10 minutes. It is a concentration effect (kap. 5): while it holds you cannot run another concentration spell, and each time you take damage you roll a Wis save DC 10 or it drops. Raising it again is a new primary action.

  • Its strength follows your caster level:

  • It is magic abs: with armour it layers as kap. 4 (Armor & natural abs), so it is worth most without armour – Abs 1 adds nothing over armour.

  • A multiclass uses the level in the caster class.

Domain powers

A wizard or sorcerer with a domain at domain level 2 (Novice) or more has that domain’s power. Like the bolt and the Ward it is not a spell: no cast roll, no slot. Using a power needs one hand free – a staff held as a focus is fine.

One domain power per round. The bolt and the Ward are domain powers too, and keeping the Ward up counts as your power for that round. Each power’s action is in the table; your other actions stay free for a weapon, a spell, a move or a parry. No domain power is a bonus action.

Range: the bolt’s – short range up to 30 m, long range 30–60 m – unless the power says otherwise.

Steps: each power grows with your caster level in four steps, 1–3, 4–6, 7–9 and 10–12. A step replaces the one before it.

Save powers – the DC. Choose 1, 2 or 3 creatures you can see, within the power’s range.

  1. Power bonus = your caster level / 2, rounded up, + Int mod (wizard) or Cha mod (sorcerer) – the same number as the bolt’s to-hit. It cannot go below 0.

  2. At long range (30–60 m) take 2 off the power bonus first.

  3. Share the power bonus between the creatures you chose, in whole numbers, as you like. Each creature must get at least 1, so you can never choose more creatures than your power bonus. With one creature it gets all of it.

  4. Each creature’s DC = 10 + its share. It rolls the save the power names against its own DC. On a success it is not affected.

  5. Each further use of a power on the same creature in the same fight gives it +5 to its save. The +5s add up: +5 the second time, +10 the third.

Clerics og Druids får deres krafter via recitering af bønner.

Fokusset for deres magi er deres hellige symbol og de kan kun lave meget begrænset magi uden.

Reciting a prayer requires the priest to hold her holy symbol – visible – that shimmer - and invoking the prayer with a clear voice.

All prayers require the priest to invoke the deities name or the name of an Avatar of the God. (Religion lore for others to know (DC9 Greater god, DC 12 intermediate, DC 15, minor, DC18 demi)

If the prayer has been cast (in the respective initiative) and the priest wants to parry an attack etc, he can draw actions from the following rounds – as per normal rules.

Kampevner

Kampevner er det man kan med våben og rustning: rustning, skjold, to våben, en enkelt klinge, afvæbning, kunstskud, slagsmål, parry, finter, rytterkamp, kommando og våben-grupper. De har intet med skills at gøre.

Man får en kampevne på en af tre måder:

  • Gratis – nogle klasser får evnen af sig selv, på level 1 eller senere.

  • Klasse-valg –

    • Rogue, paladin og ranger får ét valg på level 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11 … Et valg giver ét trin i en evne klassen må vælge.

    • The fighter picks every level

  • Købt for XP – alle andre kan købe evnen for 20 mønter per trin. Også en fighter eller rogue der vil have noget uden for sin klasse.

Trin tages i rækkefølge: I før II før III.

A step with a level in the Kræver column needs that class level. Any other step II needs class level 5 and step III class level 7. The level is the level in the class whose pick takes the step; levels in different classes are not added. A step bought with XP counts your highest class level. Trick Shooting II and Two-Weapon Fighting II need only the step before.

Uden evnen

Wizard kan kun bruge dagger og bærer ingen rustning.

Sorcerer: dagger, Leather og én våben-gruppe (se Sorcerer kampevner).

Alle andre må gerne prøve – det koster:

  • Armour heavier than your step (Leather → Studded → Chain → Plate): disadvantage on to hit, on Bal rolls (stealth, tumbling, climbing osv) and on any other roll the armour hinders. With the step you use the armour normally.

  • Skjold: giver ingen parry-bonus, disadvantage til dam(parry dam)

  • To våben: -2 med hovedhånden, -4 med off-hand.

  • Våben uden for ens våben-grupper: disadvantage på to hit.

Menace

–

Melee attacks only. Declare it before you roll the attack. If the attack beats the target’s AC by 5 or more, is not parried and does damage, the target makes a Wis save DC 15 or is Frightened of you (kap. 1) for 1 round. Each further Menace on the same opponent in one fight: +5 to its save. One Menace or Goad per round – a declared one is used even if it fails.

Goad

–

Declare it before you roll the attack. If the attack beats the target’s AC by 5 or more, is not parried and does damage, until its next turn the target has disadvantage attacking anyone but you. From the second Goad on the same opponent in one fight, it makes a Wis save DC 15 to ignore it, +5 per earlier Goad. One Menace or Goad per round – a declared one is used even if it fails.

Våben-gruppe

F, Ra: 3 på level 1, så 1 per level til alle 8 (level 6). R: Rogue-gruppen (dagger, knife, rapier, short sword, hand crossbow, short bow) – kun rogues. Pa: 1 efter valg. C: Clubs. Alle undtagen W og S: dagger, spear, crossbow. F, R, Pa, Ra: også Unarmed fra level 1. W: dagger. S: dagger + 1 efter valg.

–

Til evnerne

  • Two-Weapon Fighting: Hvis man slås med 2 våben har man et ekstra parry/attack som secondary action. The off-hand weapon adds no Str bonus to damage; a Str minus still counts. Ambidexterity (kap. 12) giver ét trin gratis – det første man ikke har.

  • Single Weapon Style: Slås med 1 våben – max medium sized. Armor uden DC mods, ellers DC mod to minus. Man skal “bruge” en secondary action per round for at aktiverer, man har ikke bonus actions når man anvender single weapon style – undtagen Swashbuckler I’s parry.

  • Expert Disarm: selve Disarm står i kap. 1 og er uændret.

  • Trick Shooting: der slås ikke – man har evnen. Fumler man, mister man angrebet.

Multiclass

Har du betalt XP for en kampevne og tager senere en klasse der får den gratis, får du XP’en tilbage. Ellers får man aldrig XP tilbage.

Eksempel: En wizard har købt Leather og Studded for 40 XP og tager et level som fighter. Fighteren får begge gratis på level 1 – wizarden får 40 XP tilbage.

Weapon groups from fighter and ranger levels are not added: each class counts its own levels (3 at its level 1, then 1 per level), and you use the larger number.

Eksempler

  • Fighter, level 5: gratis Leather, Studded, Shield og 7 våben-grupper. Five picks (one per level): Chain, Plate, Armour Training I, Shield Wall I, Riposte I. Plate at DC mod +8, +2 AC behind the shield, and a counter-attack when a parry wins by 10.

  • Rogue, level 5: gratis Leather, Two-Weapon Fighting I og Rogue-gruppen. Valg: Studded (level 1), Two-Weapon Fighting II (level 3), Expert Disarm I (level 5). To våben på 0, 0 – fx rapier og dagger. Rogue-gruppen giver også en shortbow. [FORSLAG]

  • Wizard, level 5: dagger og ingen rustning. Køber Leather og Studded for 40 XP – så bærer wizarden studded med DC mod +4, også når der castes.

Ability increase

Bought with coins. The first point you buy in an ability costs 10, and each further point in that ability costs 5 more: 10, 15, 20, 25 … Above racial max (18 + the race’s bonus) every point costs double. Example: Str 16 → 18 costs 10 + 15 = 25; the next two points, above racial max, cost (20 + 25) × 2 = 90.
