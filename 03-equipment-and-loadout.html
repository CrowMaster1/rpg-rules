Wagons

Vogne bruges ofte til transport af varer eller personer. Sjældnere som kamp-platform(chariot). Alle kan kører en vogn i gang hastighed. Skal den kører hurtigere kræver det en skill (Riding).

Hastigheden på en vogn afhænger af typen, last-vægt, terræn/klima, kuskens-egenskab, og trækdyret.

Vogn-Rejse

Vogne kan kører fast og rejser langsomt uden for gode veje.

Vogne kan generelt kun kører på veje – dvs ikke på trails og wilderness.

Rejse hastigheden forudsætter man har mennesker etc med til at få vognene op.

God vej = 100% move, sump= -95% move etc. wilderness: -75%

Herbal Rules

A Herb giving direct hitpoints, can be used once per damage taken. Takes 1 turn to use herbs on wounds. 1 round for those that stops bleeding.

In order to use a herb – it must first be found – then prepared. Herbs grow in a climate. Anyone with base herbalism can find & identify the herb with a combination of observation and herbalism. After finding the herb it must be prepared for later – see crafting.

Alchemy - Potions & Items

Alchemy rules(needs revision)

Alchemy potions are brewed in a lab. A potion is made up from agents and catalysts. The agents are usually semi-magical items – ie. monster part. The catalysts is often powdered crystals or gems. To fuse the agent and catalyst a small amount of magical energy is needed – why most alchemists usually have some form of magical talent. The difficulty of a potion is the powerscore needed to be able to brew it. A lab and time is needed.

Healing potions can only be created with the help of divine energies – ie. A priest’s prayer.
